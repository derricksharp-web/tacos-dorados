Why We Love This Dish

Tacos dorados have become a tradition at many of our family gatherings and holiday celebrations. My wife and I frequently prepare them when we have family and friends over, and they have become one of the dishes most often requested by our guests. Because of this, tacos dorados represent more to us than simply a favorite food. They have become connected with spending time with family and friends and sharing something that we enjoy making together.

The recipe also has a connection to my wife's family and their roots in Jalisco, Mexico, where this style of taco is commonly prepared. Our version combines that influence with the flavors and preparation methods that we enjoy. We prepare the tacos with a chorizo and potato filling and serve them with optional toppings such as shredded cabbage, Mexican crema, salsa, and lime.

Tacos dorados are also practical for gatherings. They are inexpensive to prepare in large quantities, easy to scale up or down, and use ingredients that are easy to find. Most of the preparation can also be completed in advance, making them a convenient option for both large gatherings and quick meals.
