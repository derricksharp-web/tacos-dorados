Homemade Salsas

These are two of our favorite salsa recipes to serve with tacos dorados. Choose the salsa that best matches your preferred level of heat.

Salsa Verde (Mild to Medium)

Ingredients

  • 5 tomatillos
  • 2 jalapeños
  • 1 serrano pepper
  • ½ white onion
  • 1 tablespoon powdered chicken bouillon
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 1 cup water

Directions

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add the tomatillos, jalapeños, and serrano pepper.
  3. Cook until the ingredients have softened. The tomatillos will generally soften before the peppers, so remove the ingredients as they finish cooking.
  4. Set the cooked tomatillos and peppers aside and allow them to cool to room temperature.
  5. Add the cooled tomatillos and peppers to a blender along with the onion, chicken bouillon, cilantro, and water.
  6. Blend until smooth.
  7. Taste and adjust as desired before serving.

Salsa Roja (Hot)

Ingredients

  • 7–10 tomatillos
  • 3 dried California chiles
  • 10–20 dried chiles de árbol, depending on desired heat
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 teaspoon salt to start, then adjust to taste

Directions

  1. Place the tomatillos in a dry pan and roast them until softened and lightly charred on all sides.
  2. Remove the seeds from the dried California chiles.
  3. Using low heat, lightly toast the California chiles and chiles de árbol for approximately 10–20 seconds per side. Do not allow the dried chiles to burn or char because this can make the salsa bitter.
  4. Add the toasted dried chiles and ½ cup of water to a blender. Blend on high until the chiles are thoroughly broken down and the mixture is smooth.
  5. Add the roasted tomatillos and 1 teaspoon of salt.
  6. For a chunky salsa, pulse briefly until the desired consistency is reached. For a smooth salsa, blend on high until smooth.
  7. Taste the finished salsa and adjust the salt as needed.
